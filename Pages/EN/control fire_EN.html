Control Fire

A small, starting fire is often the moment when fast action makes all the difference. Our fire extinguishing spray is specifically designed for that crucial moment: simple, safe and effective. Whether it’s in the kitchen, at a BBQ, in a caravan or with small electrical appliances, this spray keeps you prepared. Its lightweight, compact design makes it ideal for everyday use. The operation is straightforward, allowing anyone to use it safely – even in stressful situations.

For small fires at home

This innovative fire extinguishing spray is your first line of defense against starting fires. Thanks to its impressive cooling capacity up to 1,000 °C, it is ideal for use in the kitchen, during barbecues, on camping trips, DIY work and other high-risk situations. It quickly and effectively extinguishes fires in classes A, B, D, F and electrical equipment up to 1,000 V. This helps limit damage and protects you, your family and your home.

100% biodegradable and non-toxic

Contains no harmful, toxic or corrosive substances. Thanks to powerful plant-based extracts, the spray is highly effective. Safe for the environment, your family and pets. Completely PFAS-free.

No powder residue, targeted and easy to use

The spray leaves no powder residue and is easy to rinse off with clean water. Thanks to its targeted spray system, you can extinguish fires safely and accurately from a distance of 2–3 meters, without unnecessary mess or risk.

Certified and proven effective

Approved by ACEG, which assessed the spray as effective for starting fires and confirmed that temperature and smoke development are significantly reduced. Recognized by NORMEC as 100% biodegradable, non-toxic and safe to use.
