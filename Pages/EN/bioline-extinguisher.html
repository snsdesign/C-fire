BIOLINE fire extinguisher

The BIOLINE fire extinguisher from C-fire is a 6-liter extinguisher of the latest generation. We go a step further than PFAS-free. Instead of foam or powder, it uses a water-based solution with plant extracts. This makes BIOLINE 100% free from harmful chemicals, frost-resistant and suitable for outdoor use.
