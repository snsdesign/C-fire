C-Fire: The specialist in environmentally friendly fire protection.

Who are we? →

C-Fire manufactures innovative and eco-friendly fire fighting solutions and systems. Our key principle is: even in the world of fire safety it is possible to help tackle climate change. This is why we develop high quality products that minimize the impact on humans, animals and the environment as well.

Our entire product range meets the most stringent Belgian and European standards and can, if needed, be completely customized. Furthermore, C-Fire takes care of the installation, annual maintenance and inspection of all fire extinguishers and wall-mounted fire hose reels in accordance with the Belgian NBN S21-050 and NBN EN 671-3 standards.

C-Fire is a part of the Belgian Cordeel Group.

Which fire extinguisher do I need?

Not every fire requires the same solution. Depending on the environment and the level of risk, it is best to choose a fire extinguisher that fits your situation. We are happy to guide you.

For home, vehicle or travel

For small, early-stage fires at home, in a caravan, car or on the go, a compact solution is ideal.

Recommended: Control Fire

Control Fire →

For offices and public spaces

In offices, shops and public environments, a user-friendly and clean solution is essential.

Recommended: foam extinguishers

Foam extinguishers →

For industry and workshops

In industrial environments or workshops, higher risks are often present. A powerful and versatile solution is required.

Recommended: powder extinguishers

Powder extinguishers →

For electrical installations

In case of fire in switch cabinets, server rooms or electrical equipment, an extinguisher is needed that does not cause additional damage.

Recommended: CO₂ extinguishers
